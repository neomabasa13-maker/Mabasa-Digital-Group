How a project works

  1. Discovery

    You explain your business, goals, audience and requirements.

  2. Planning and design

    Scope, structure, content needs and visual direction are agreed.

  3. Development and testing

    The website is built, tested and refined.

  4. Launch and support

    Once approved, the site goes live and any agreed support is explained.
